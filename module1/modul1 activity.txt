Classroom Activities

Activity 1: How the Internet Works

Students simulate the interaction between a browser, server, and
database to understand how requests are processed and responses are
returned.

Activity 2: Logic Building Challenge

Students solve progressively complex logical problems using flowcharts
and pseudocode before implementing them in code.

Activity 3: Real-world Scenario Mapping

Students identify common applications they use daily and map their
frontend, backend, and database components.
